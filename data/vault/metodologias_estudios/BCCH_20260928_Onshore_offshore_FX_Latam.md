# Final remarks

###### ❖The Chilean peso has become an increasingly
 international currency
 ➢ More than half of FX activity takes place offshore, supported by
 significant participation from international market participants ❖Chile's FX market is among the deepest in Latin
 America
 ➢ Both onshore and total FX turnover compare favorably with regional
 peers relative to economic size ❖Offshore activity is an important component of the
 CLP market
 ➢ Offshore activity represents a substantial share of CLP trading and
 should be considered when evaluating market size, structure, and cross- border participation
